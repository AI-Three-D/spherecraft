# Accepted river-bank direction

2026-10-17. User direction for the river checkpoint after the lake cache step:

- Raise ground only where the bank beside the channel is below the river's water.
- Target the water level plus approximately 0.3 m, with a narrow footprint a few metres wide and a smooth blend into natural ground.
- Avoid long walls. Measure required rise before accepting a change.
- Where required rise exceeds roughly 1–1.5 m, test lowering the local river level instead of building a higher wall.
- Report raised-bank total length, the height distribution, maximum rise and worst locations, with screenshots.
- Require no hanging river edges at shot 5, the closed pocket to remain dry, and no o4-star regression.

These constraints authorize testing narrow selective fill and local level
reduction where needed. They do not authorize unconditional bank raising or
accept a one-metre rise estimate without measurements. This checkpoint has
not changed river terrain or water levels.

For the outlet, replace reliance on the old coarse lake band with measured
bank containment using final heights. Verify a complete shared cross-section
where lake and river heights agree, and verify that lake coverage cannot route
around it. Sampling two bank points alone does not establish that. Account for
any change in final bank heights caused by the proposed level or bank edits.
