# Visual references

## visual-target-2026-11-03.png: the minimum bar for the whole planet

The owner, 2026-11-03: "This is just about the bare minimum we will need to
have at the end. Vast forests, rivers, lakes, mountains, etc. Preferably much
much better than what this pic even has. Of course it's not just the rivers
that are lacking and we will work on those later."

It is a general target for the landscape, not only for water. What it shows
(aerial view, a few kilometres across):

- **Mountains:** sharp, rugged granite ranges with snow-dusted ridges and
  steep faces, rising straight out of the lowland; isolated rocky massifs
  and outcrops scattered between them.
- **Lowland between the ranges:** green meadow and plateau land, broken by
  many small rocky knolls; not flat, not uniformly smooth.
- **Lakes:** many, of very different sizes, close together; irregular
  organic shores with bays, points and islands; chained into one another.
- **Rivers:** meandering channels connecting the lakes, narrow streams
  between nearby lakes, a broad river with smooth curving banks; water
  reads as one continuous body from river into lake.
- **Forests:** dense conifer forests in large patches and along valleys,
  leaving meadows and rock open.
- **Atmosphere:** strong aerial perspective; haze toward the horizon;
  warm sunlight with clear shadows on the relief.
